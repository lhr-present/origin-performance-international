01 // About

Performance Coaching
Built on Elite Experience

Championship Podium

Oarigin Performance International is a rowing performance coaching company based in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida. We work with youth and junior athletes, their families, and rowing clubs, offering individual and group coaching in person and online.

Our coaching combines experience gained at the highest levels of international competition with a structured, athlete-centered development process. We coach across sculling and sweep disciplines, with a clear focus on technique, physical preparation, and a competitive mindset.

Athletes coached by our staff have become consistent podium finishers at the Florida State Rowing Association Championships and the USRowing Southeast Youth Championships, have medaled at the USRowing Youth National Championships, and in 2024 won Gold for the US National Team at CanAmex. Our athletes have also gone on to row at NCAA Division I programs, including the University of Tennessee.

Our goal is to help each athlete reach their full potential and to prepare them for a long future in the sport.

  • Coaching led by a former national team athlete with 10+ years of international competition
  • Youth and junior development across sculling and sweep
  • Championship results at the FSRA State Championships (2023–2026), USRowing Southeast Youth, and USRowing Youth Nationals (2023–2025)
  • CanAmex US National Team, Women's Youth 4x Gold (2024)
  • Athlete placements in NCAA Division I rowing, including the University of Tennessee
  • In-person coaching in South Florida and online coaching worldwide

03 // Our Approach

Our Approach

“Every athlete has a ceiling they cannot see yet. Our job is to help them break through it — technically, physically, and mentally.”
◎

Athlete-First Development

Every program is built around the individual: their physiology, timeline, and goals. There is no universal template. Our coaching starts with understanding each athlete.

◈

Technical Mastery

Rowing is a technique sport before it is a fitness sport. Clean, efficient movement built on solid fundamentals is the non-negotiable foundation. Power built on poor mechanics is wasted potential.

◇

Long-Term Thinking

Results matter, but so does what comes after. Our athletes have progressed to NCAA Division I programs and US National Team selection, and many build a lifelong relationship with the sport. We prioritize sustainable development over short-term gains.
